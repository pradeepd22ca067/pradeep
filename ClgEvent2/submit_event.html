Organize Your Own Campus Event! 🎉

Ready to bring your idea to life? Fill out the proposal form below. The Event Committee reviews all submissions weekly.

Submission Guidelines

  • **Proposal Details:** Ensure you include the event name, date, time, and detailed description.
  • **Required Info:** Provide the organizing body/club name, contact details (Email, Phone), and estimated budget.
  • **Approval Timeline:** Submit at least **3 weeks** before your proposed event date for review.
  • **Venue:** Specify your preferred location and an alternative.
  • **Promotion:** Detail your planned promotional strategies.

Event Proposal Form
